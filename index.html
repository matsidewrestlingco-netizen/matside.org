Wrestling-native — not a vendor with a wrestling product line.

Matside Wrestling Co. is founded and operated by Daniel Emmons. We run events and build software from inside the sport — partnering with tournament directors who carry event-level insurance and own volunteer coordination, while we bring the bracket, scoring, and live-results infrastructure.

04Leadership

Who runs Matside

DE
Founder & Operator · USA Bracketing Expert

Daniel Emmons

Daniel Emmons, Owner and President of Matside Wrestling Co., has been involved in the sport since his elementary school days in Cincinnati, Ohio. As a father of wrestlers and an active coach and booster, Daniel recognized the need for better tools in the wrestling community — and built Matside to fill that gap.

Matside Wrestling Co. operates across two areas: tournament facilitation and wrestling-specific software development. Grounded in firsthand experience, the company is uniquely positioned to create solutions that move the sport forward.

Based near Pittsburgh — the heart of wrestling country — Daniel lives with his wife Celia, children Will, Ana, and West, and their dogs George and Dayton.
