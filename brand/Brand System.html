One parent. Two sub-products. Shared DNA.

Matside Wrestling Co. is the parent brand and the visual anchor. SignupSignin and MatPass are sub-products with their own personalities — but they inherit Matside's spine: Midnight Navy, Steel Blue, Inter, and a flat, technical layout language.

EXCLUSIVITY RULES

  • 01Mat Red — MatPass only. Never appears in other product UI.
  • 02Royal Blue (#2E4A7D) — SignupSignin only. Distinct from Matside's Steel Blue.
  • 03Signal Amber (#F59E0B) — WrestleFA only. Never paired with Mat Red.

04 Typography
scale FAMILIES 3 UI BODY Inter (universal)

Inter is family. Display fonts shift per product.

All three products share Inter for body and UI — that's the unifying thread. Display fonts diverge to express each brand's personality: Montserrat for Matside & SignupSignin (geometric, modern), Anton for MatPass (heavy, urgent, compliance voice).

05 Voice &
tone SHARED Direct · No fluff VARIES Warmth · urgency

One spine. Three temperatures.

All three brands speak directly and avoid fluff. They differ in temperature: Matside is confident and competitive, SignupSignin is helpful and warm, MatPass is urgent and absolute. The same writer should be able to draft for any of them by adjusting one dial.

SHARED RULES

  • 01Lead with the outcome, not the feature
  • 02Sentences under 14 words wherever possible
  • 03Active voice; second person ("you") when addressing users
  • 04No emoji in product UI. No exclamation points outside MatPass alerts.

WHEN IN DOUBT

  • 01Matside → does it sound like a coach, not a marketer?
  • 02SignupSignin → could a tired volunteer parent read this?
  • 03MatPass → would a referee trust this?
  • 04If the line could appear in any of the three, it's probably too generic. Sharpen it.
06 WrestleFA
showcase STATUS Proposal v1 PROMISE Visibility + match speed

WrestleFA — the free-agent draft board.

WrestleFA matches free-agent wrestlers with teams and tournament boards. The brand pulls from the Matside spine — Midnight Navy, Inter, flat technical layouts — and adds Signal Amber as its signature accent: the color of "available now" lights on a draft board. Athlete-empowering voice ("Get seen. Get matched."), recruitment-platform polish, draft-day urgency.
