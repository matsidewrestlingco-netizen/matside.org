SIGNUPSIGNIN

One-Page Brand Kit

⸻

🧭 Brand Positioning

SignupSignin is a streamlined, user-first platform built to simplify volunteer coordination and event management. It emphasizes clarity, efficiency, and accessibility for both organizers and participants.

Core Attributes:

* Simple
* Efficient
* Friendly
* Reliable

⸻

🎨 Color Palette

Primary Colors

* Primary Blue (Brand Base) — #2E4A7D
    Establishes trust, stability, and professionalism
* Soft White — #F3F4F6
    Clean, accessible text and UI contrast

Accent Colors

* Light Blue Highlight — #93C5FD
    Used for emphasis and secondary highlights
* Success Green — #22C55E
    Used for confirmations, check-ins, and positive actions

Supporting Colors

* Slate Gray — #64748B
    Secondary text and UI elements
* Deep Blue (Hover/Active) — #1E3A5F
    Hover states and active interactions

⸻

🔤 Typography

Headline Font

Inter (Bold / SemiBold)

* Clean, modern, and highly legible
* Used for hero text and key messaging

Body & UI Font

Inter (Regular / Medium)

* Consistent across UI for simplicity
* Optimized for readability in dashboards and forms

⸻

🧱 Logo Usage

Primary Logo

* Vertical stacked “signUp / signIn” with directional arrows

Guidelines

* Maintain clear spacing around logo
* Use white logo on dark backgrounds
* Avoid distortion or color changes outside palette

⸻

🎯 UI & Button Styling

* Primary buttons: Soft White background (#F3F4F6) with dark text
* Secondary buttons: Primary Blue (#2E4A7D) with white text
* Hover state: Deep Blue (#1E3A5F)
* Rounded corners: medium (6–10px)

⸻

📦 Component Style

* Cards use slightly lighter blue than background
* Subtle shadows or elevation for separation
* Icons are simple, emoji-style or minimal line icons

⸻

📸 Visual Style

* Clean, minimal layouts
* Soft gradients in blue tones
* Focus on usability over decoration

⸻

🗣 Tone & Voice

* Clear
* Helpful
* Straightforward
* Low friction

Example:
“Organize your volunteers without the chaos.”

⸻

⚠️ Do / Don’t

Do

* Prioritize clarity and ease of use
* Keep layouts spacious and readable
* Use color to guide actions

Don’t

* Overcomplicate UI with too many elements
* Use harsh contrasts that reduce readability
* Add unnecessary branding noise

⸻

🚀 Brand Direction (Next Steps)

* Introduce a more distinct icon system (less emoji reliance)
* Refine gradients for stronger visual identity
* Build a design system for scaling across web and mobile
