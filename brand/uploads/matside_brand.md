# MATSIDE WRESTLING CO.

## One-Page Brand Kit

---

## 🧭 Brand Positioning

Matside Wrestling Co. is a modern, performance-driven wrestling brand focused on competition, operations, and athlete development. The brand balances professionalism with grit—built for serious athletes, coaches, and organizers.

**Core Attributes:**

* Competitive
* Clean
* Modern
* Tough (without being loud)

---

## 🎨 Color Palette

### Primary Colors

* **Midnight Navy** — #0A0F1A
  Primary background color; establishes depth and seriousness

* **Off-White** — #E5E7EB
  Primary text color; softer than pure white for readability

### Accent Color

* **Steel Blue** — #3B82C4
  Signature brand color; used for emphasis, buttons, and highlights

### Supporting Colors

* **Muted Gray** — #6B7280
  Secondary text and subtle UI elements

* **Deep Blue (Hover/Active)** — #2F6FA3
  Used for hover states and interactive elements

---

## 🔤 Typography

### Headline Font

**Montserrat** (Bold / SemiBold)

* Used for logos, headlines, and key messaging
* Strong, geometric, modern

### Body & UI Font

**Inter** (Regular / Medium)

* Used for navigation, paragraphs, and UI elements
* Clean and highly readable across devices

---

## 🧱 Logo Usage

### Primary Logo

* “MATSIDE” in Off-White
* “WRESTLING CO.” in Steel Blue

### Guidelines

* Maintain high contrast (light text on dark background preferred)
* Avoid placing logo on busy or low-contrast backgrounds
* Do not distort, stretch, or recolor outside the palette

---

## 🎯 UI & Button Styling

* Buttons use **Steel Blue (#3B82C4)** background
* Button text: Off-White (#E5E7EB)
* Hover state: Deep Blue (#2F6FA3)
* Rounded corners: subtle (4–8px)

---

## 📸 Visual Style

* Dark, high-contrast backgrounds
* Minimal clutter
* Focus on athletes, mats, and action
* Lighting should emphasize intensity and movement

---

## 🗣 Tone & Voice

* Direct
* Confident
* No fluff
* Performance-focused

**Example:**
“Run better tournaments. Build better wrestlers.”

---

## ⚠️ Do / Don’t

### Do

* Keep layouts clean and structured
* Use blue strategically (not everywhere)
* Prioritize readability and hierarchy

### Don’t

* Overuse colors outside the palette
* Add unnecessary effects or gradients
* Make it look like a youth flyer—this is a serious brand

---
